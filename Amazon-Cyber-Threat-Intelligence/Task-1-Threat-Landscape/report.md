# Task 1 – Sector & Threat Landscape Profiling

## Simulated Organization
Amazon

## Objective

This task profiles the cyber threat landscape relevant to Amazon as a technology, e-commerce, and cloud-services organization. The investigation was conducted using OpenCTI to examine relevant threats, threat actors, malware, campaigns, and attack techniques.

## Sector Profile

Amazon operates across technology, e-commerce, and cloud computing. Its large digital infrastructure, online services, customer accounts, cloud environments, and extensive supply-chain ecosystem create multiple areas of potential exposure to cyber threats.

## Threat Landscape

The OpenCTI investigation focused on identifying relationships between the organization, threat actors, malware, campaigns, vulnerabilities, and attack techniques.

Key areas examined included:

- Threat actors targeting technology and online-service environments
- Malware and tools associated with intrusion activity
- Campaigns and reported attack activity
- MITRE ATT&CK techniques associated with identified threats
- Infrastructure and indicators connected to threat activity
- Victim and targeting relationships

## Threat Actors and Malware

The investigation included examination of threat intelligence entities such as ModeloRAT and TraderTraitor, together with their associated relationships and activity recorded in the CTI platform.

These entities were investigated to understand how threat actors, malware, campaigns, infrastructure, and techniques can be connected during CTI analysis.

## MITRE ATT&CK Relevance

MITRE ATT&CK provides a structured framework for understanding adversary behavior. Techniques identified during the investigation can be mapped to ATT&CK tactics and techniques to support detection, investigation, and defensive planning.

## Targeting Patterns

The investigation considered common targeting areas relevant to a large technology organization, including:

- Cloud and internet-facing infrastructure
- User accounts and credentials
- Corporate endpoints
- Web applications and services
- Third-party and supply-chain environments
- Data and customer-facing systems

## Intelligence Value

OpenCTI provides relationship-based analysis that allows analysts to connect threat actors, malware, campaigns, infrastructure, victims, and attack techniques. This supports a more structured understanding of the threat landscape and can help prioritize further investigation.

## Evidence

Screenshots from the OpenCTI investigation will be placed in the Task 1 evidence directory. The screenshots document the entities, relationships, searches, and analysis used during the investigation.

## Conclusion

Task 1 established a structured threat landscape for the simulated Amazon environment. The investigation provides the foundation for the subsequent country-level threat assessment, victim and threat deep dive, and political threat actor analysis.
