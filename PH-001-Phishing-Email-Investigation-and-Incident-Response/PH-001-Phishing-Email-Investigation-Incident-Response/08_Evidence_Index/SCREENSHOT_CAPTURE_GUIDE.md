# PH-001 Screenshot and Evidence Guide

## Purpose
This guide explains the organization of screenshots supporting the phishing investigation.

## Screenshot Categories

### 01_Email_Triage
- Simulated phishing email
- Sender and recipient details
- Subject and suspicious URL

### 02_Email_Header_Analysis
- MXToolbox header analysis
- DNS and email authentication lookup results

### 03_URL_Domain_Investigation
- VirusTotal URL analysis
- IP address and passive DNS information
- DNSChecker results
- ICANN RDAP information
- CyberChef URL analysis

### 04_Endpoint_Investigation
- Historical DOCX and LNK threat intelligence
- Simulated endpoint process activity
- Simulated PowerShell and command execution records

### 06_Incident_Response
- Simulated containment records
- Incident response investigation evidence

## Evidence Handling
- Preserve original screenshots where possible.
- Do not alter evidence in a way that changes its meaning.
- Clearly distinguish historical threat intelligence from simulated records.
- Do not present training records as evidence from a real corporate environment.
